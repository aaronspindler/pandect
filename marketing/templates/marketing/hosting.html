Before you begin

A few practical questions.

Do I need an AI service?

No. Cases, evidence, search, imports, maps, and local checks work without one. Transcription and model-assisted analysis require separately configured services.

Can analysis stay on my infrastructure?

Supported AI defaults to configured local or private-LAN runtimes, with no cloud fallback. External AI requires explicit selection and approval. Maps have separate privacy settings. See processing and privacy.

Where do I sign in?

Use your own or your operator’s installation. This site’s sign-in opens accounts on this installation.

How do I update and back up?

Follow the operations guide for upgrades, backups, and recovery. Check compatibility requirements before updating.
