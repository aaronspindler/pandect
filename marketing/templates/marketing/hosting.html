Before you begin

A few practical questions.

What works without an AI service?

Case management, evidence collection, search, maps of recorded locations, imports, and supported local checks and reports. Printed-English OCR runs locally. Transcription and model-assisted analysis need separately configured runtimes. See service requirements.

Can analysis stay on my infrastructure?

Supported AI tools default to operator-configured local or private-LAN runtimes. Local failures never trigger cloud fallback. External AI uses a separately configured Cloudflare AI Gateway route and requires explicit selection and approval. Map tiles and geographic endpoints have their own configuration. Read about processing and privacy.

Where do I create an account or sign in?

Use the installation you or your operator has set up. Accounts and case access belong to that installation. The sign-in link on this site opens its account flow.

How do I update and back up Pandect?

Follow the operations guide for deployment, upgrades, backups, and recovery. Pandect is in active development; check compatibility requirements before updating.
