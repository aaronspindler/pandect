Evidence and privacy

Keep control.
Keep the source.

Control who can see a case, trace findings to evidence, and choose where processing happens.

Access stays with the case.

One owner manages invitations and viewer or editor access. Viewing, downloads, search, and background analysis require current case access, including on self-hosted installations.

Originals remain originals.

Originals stay separate from findings and review decisions. Checksums track integrity, not truth.

Archiving retains evidence. Owners can confirm whole-case deletion; individual deletion requires a superuser with current case access and a confirmed inventory.

Evidence and retention guide ↗

Findings are reviewable.

Findings retain source references, processing versions, and review history. Observations, patterns, and AI suggestions remain distinct. Possible trips and matching identifiers do not prove routes, identity, or association.

Processing choices

Know where your material goes.

Local checks

Supported text extraction, metadata checks, and printed-English image recognition run locally. Image OCR has no external fallback.

Private AI services

Other supported AI defaults to configured local or private-LAN runtimes. Missing or failed local processing never triggers cloud fallback.

External AI by approval

External AI requires a configured Cloudflare AI Gateway, explicit selection, and approval. Operators manage logging and retention.

Maps have privacy choices, too.

Configure private tiles and geographic services. External address and context lookups require approval unless designated local.

Viewing external map tiles shares your browser’s IP address, site origin, and viewed areas with the tile provider.
