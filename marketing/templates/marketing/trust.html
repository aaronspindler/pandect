01 / Case access

Who can access a case?

One owner manages invitations and viewer or editor access. Viewing, downloads, search, and background analysis require current case access, including on self-hosted installations.

02 / Evidence integrity

How are originals preserved?

Originals stay separate from derived findings. Checksums help verify that retained content matches its recorded version.

Archiving retains evidence. Owners can confirm whole-case deletion. Individual deletion requires a superuser with current case access and a confirmed inventory.

03 / Provenance

How do I trace a finding?

Review its source references and processing versions. Pandect distinguishes source observations, detected patterns, and AI suggestions. Treat possible trips and matching identifiers as leads to review against the evidence.

Evidence processing

Where is evidence processed?

Eligible analysis starts automatically after evidence is added. Where it runs depends on the method, the installation’s configured services, and the case owner’s approvals.

Included

Local checks

Supported text extraction, metadata checks, and printed-English image recognition run locally. Image OCR stays in the local parser.

Requires configuration

Private AI services

Operators can configure local or private-LAN services for supported AI methods. A private service failure leaves that check unavailable.

Requires approval

External AI

Operators can connect OpenAI, Anthropic, or Cloudflare AI Gateway for text analysis. A service can receive evidence only after the case owner approves its configured destination in Case Settings.

Geographic services

What do map services receive?

Operators can configure private tiles and geographic services. Case owners approve destinations configured as external before Pandect sends address or context lookups.

Viewing external map tiles shares your browser’s IP address, site origin, and viewed areas with the tile provider.
