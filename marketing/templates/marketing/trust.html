01 / Case access

The case is the boundary.

One owner manages invitations and viewer or editor access. Viewing, downloads, search, and background analysis require current case access, including on self-hosted installations.

02 / Evidence integrity

Originals remain originals.

Originals stay separate from derived findings. Checksums track integrity, not truth.

Archiving retains evidence. Owners can confirm whole-case deletion; individual deletion requires a superuser with current case access and a confirmed inventory.

03 / Provenance

A finding is a starting point.

Findings retain source references and processing versions. Observations, patterns, and AI suggestions remain distinct. Possible trips and matching identifiers do not prove routes, identity, or association.

Processing choices

Know where
your material goes.

Use the tools your investigation needs, with the destination and approval visible before external processing.

Included

Local checks

Supported text extraction, metadata checks, and printed-English image recognition run locally. Image OCR has no external fallback.

Requires configuration

Private AI services

Use configured local or private-LAN services for supported AI. Processing stays on the reviewed route; failures never trigger cloud fallback.

Requires approval

External AI

Operators can connect OpenAI, Anthropic, or Cloudflare AI Gateway for text analysis. Investigators review and approve the destination before sending evidence.

Geographic services

Location context.
Informed choices.

Configure private tiles and geographic services. External address and context lookups require approval unless designated local.

Viewing external map tiles shares your browser’s IP address, site origin, and viewed areas with the tile provider.
