Evidence and privacy

Know who can access your case, where a finding came from, and when evidence is sent to an external AI service.

Originals stay intact

Pandect keeps original files and captured text separate from source metadata and analysis. Source details and checksums help you trace what was received.

Archived evidence stays available to case members. Read the evidence guide for how retention and deletion work.

Access is set per case

Owners choose who can review or edit each case. Opening evidence or downloading a file requires current access to that case.

Findings link to sources

Review the evidence behind an analysis result. Recorded observations, derived findings, and review decisions stay distinct from the original. A possible connection is a lead you can assess.

See analysis results and their sources →

External AI requires approval

Printed English text recognition runs locally. Other AI tools default to a local or private service that the operator configures. Pandect sends evidence to an external AI service only when that route is configured, selected, and approved.

See hosting options →

Map tile privacy

When an operator configures external map tiles, opening a map shares your browser’s IP address, the site origin, and the map areas viewed with the tile provider. Read about map privacy.
