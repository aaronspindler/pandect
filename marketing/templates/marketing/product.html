Supported formats

Know what can be analyzed.

Storage supports more formats than automated analysis. Results disclose unsupported inputs and incomplete coverage.

Documents and images

Extract embedded text from unencrypted PDFs and UTF-8 files. Local OCR reads printed English in JPEG/PNG images. Handwriting, Office extraction, and OCR directly from scanned PDF pages are unsupported; use a separate page image.

Audio and plate readings

Audio analysis supports the documented uncompressed 16-bit PCM WAV profile; transcription supports English. Local plate detection in JPEG/PNG can miss plates or misread text. It identifies neither an owner nor a driver.

Imports and report coverage

Imports support documented export profiles, with timezone and association review, rather than live tracking feeds. Saved vehicle trips process complete eligible histories; other reports use at most 500 source records and disclose truncation and gaps.
