Available now

The foundation

  • Private case work. Retained evidence, people, vehicles, viewer/editor access, and search within a case.
  • Source-linked analysis. Supported local checks, reviewable findings, exact identifier candidates, possible trips, and recorded chronology.
  • Maps and imports. Possible trips, points of interest, supported vehicle CSV, message CSV, and iMessage HTML imports.
  • Shared briefs. Saved evidence and finding references, printable briefs, and provenance manifests.
  • Optional services. Transcription, model suggestions, synthesis over selected text/findings, and geographic lookups require configuration.

Planned · Not yet available

A wider case view

  • Inferred timelines. Reviewable events drawn from narrative evidence.
  • Relationships and graphs. Evidence-backed exploration beyond exact identifier matches.
  • Cross-case evidence search. Search material across cases you can access.
  • Broader discovery. Retrieve supporting synthesis material without selecting every input.
  • Managed hosting. A service at openpandect.com, with timing and pricing unannounced.
