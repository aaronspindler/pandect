Project roadmap

What’s here.
What’s ahead.

Pandect is being built incrementally. The workspace already supports real case workflows; the wider investigation platform is still developing.

Plans describe direction, with no committed release dates. Priorities and scope may change.

Available now

The working foundation

Implemented in the current application.

  • Cases and collaboration. Owner-managed access, viewers, and editors.
  • Evidence and search. Retained originals, source details, and search within a case.
  • Findings and reports. Supported local extraction, metadata, text checks, possible plate readings, vehicle trips, call patterns, and duplicate files.
  • Maps and imports. Recorded locations, person points of interest, supported vehicle CSV and iMessage HTML imports.
  • Optional services. Transcription, model suggestions, and geographic lookups with separate configuration.
  • Self-hosting. Docker Compose setup and operator documentation.

Planned · Not yet available

Connect the wider case

The next product direction, rather than a release commitment.

  • Unified case timeline. Review events from different sources in one chronological view.
  • Relationships and graphs. Explore possible links between records and inspect the evidence behind each connection.
  • Cross-case evidence search. Search material across cases you can access.
  • Case-wide synthesis. Build a broader case summary with claims traceable to their sources.
  • Managed hosting. A planned service at openpandect.com. Launch timing and pricing are unannounced.

Exploring · Not yet available

Extend supported analysis

Ideas that need further design and evaluation.

  • Related passages. Retrieve text by meaning alongside existing text search.
  • Structured documents. Extract tables and forms while preserving their structure and source locations.
  • Similar images. Flag visual near-duplicates for review, beyond today’s exact-file matching.
  • Broader media support. Extend supported formats and evaluate video processing.
