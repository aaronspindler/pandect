Trust & evidence

Good investigations start with careful foundations.

Know where material came from, who can access it, and what a result actually tells you.

01 / PRESERVATION

Keep the original.

Original files and captured content are retained separately from editable descriptions and analysis. Source details and checksums help you trace what was received.

Archiving evidence keeps it available to current case members. Owners can confirm deletion of a whole case. Case-authorized superusers can also confirm individual evidence deletion after reviewing the original and derived material that will be removed.

02 / PROVENANCE

Keep the trail.

Derived results retain source references and processing history. Reviews and corrections add context without silently rewriting original material.

A checksum helps assess integrity. A source reference helps trace a claim. Neither establishes that the underlying claim is true.

03 / JUDGMENT

Keep the distinction.

Observations, investigator notes, and model-generated suggestions serve different purposes. Pandect keeps those distinctions visible so you can review the evidence behind a result.

A shared identifier or apparent connection is a lead to assess, not proof of identity, ownership, or causation.

Access follows the case

Share deliberately.

Each case has one owner. Invitations grant access to that case, and subsequent requests check current permissions, including private downloads.

Owner

Manages collaborators and their roles, contributes to the investigation, and can delete the case.

Editor

Adds evidence and edits investigative work within the shared case.

Viewer

Inspects case material and downloads retained originals without editing the investigation.

Deliberate processing

Know when evidence
leaves your environment.

Printed-image text recognition runs locally. Other supported model-assisted analysis defaults to an explicitly configured local or private runtime.

External AI processing requires deliberate selection and approval through the configured gateway. Pandect does not silently fall back to an external AI service.

Storage, backups, service configuration, and any external processing policies depend on the deployment operator. Hosted-service terms and operational commitments will be published before launch.
