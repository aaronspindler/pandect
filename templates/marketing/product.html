01 / THE CASE

Everything belongs
somewhere.

Bring files, captured text, locations, and communications into a shared case. Add people and vehicles as the work develops, and keep source details attached to the material they describe.

  • Documents, images, audio, and original text
  • Messages, calls, and retained call attachments
  • People, vehicles, and recorded locations
  • Archive and restore without replacing originals
02 / ANALYSIS

Read the content.
Explore the patterns.

Extract searchable text, explore conversations, compare writing, and analyze reported locations. Results link to the evidence so you can check each lead.

  • Text extraction from supported PDFs and text files
  • Local printed-English image text recognition
  • Text keywords and writing comparisons
  • Call patterns, location coverage, and speed reports

Extraction depends on the source and format. Model-assisted features require an operator-configured runtime or an explicitly approved external route.

03 / THE PEOPLE

A shared case.
Clear responsibilities.

Invite people into the cases they need. Viewers can inspect the material; editors can add evidence and edit investigative work. One owner manages access and case deletion.

People recorded in an investigation are separate from the accounts collaborating on it. A name, identifier, or connection remains something to assess.
