A few useful answers

Before you begin.

Is hosted Pandect available now?

Not yet. openpandect.com is the planned home for the hosted service. Launch timing, pricing, service terms, and support details have not been announced.

Can I use Pandect on my own infrastructure?

Yes. The repository includes Docker Compose setup and operational guidance. You are responsible for deployment, maintenance, backups, and recovery. Read the operations guide.

Do I need an AI service to use Pandect?

No. Ordinary case management, evidence storage, and collaboration work without AI credentials. Supported model-assisted analysis requires separate operator configuration.

I already have an account. Where do I sign in?

Sign in on the Pandect deployment where your account was created. Sign in to this deployment. An account on a self-hosted installation is separate from the future hosted service.
