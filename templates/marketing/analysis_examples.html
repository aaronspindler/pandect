More analysis with configured services

Transcription and model suggestions

With the appropriate private runtime configured, you can transcribe English speech in supported WAV recordings, suggest topics, claims and commitments, and summarize conversations or compare exchanges by meaning. External AI requires a configured gateway, deliberate selection, and approval.

Geographic lookup and context

A configured geocoder can look up candidate addresses or coordinates. A separate map reference service can show nearby roads, buildings, or places. Review estimates before using them in other analyses. External lookups require approval.
