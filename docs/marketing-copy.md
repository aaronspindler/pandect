## Short description

Collect files, messages, and location records in a private case. Pandect retains
original evidence and links findings to their sources. Invite viewers or editors
to review records, recorded activity, and possible trips together. Pandect is
self-hostable and in active development.

## Project introduction

Pandect is a self-hostable investigation workspace built with Django and
PostgreSQL. Organize collected material in private cases with their own people,
vehicles, evidence, and permissions. Inspect the originals behind findings,
repeated identifiers, and recorded chronology.

Pandect performs built-in checks such as native document text extraction on the
installation. Case owners can enable configured Cloudflare services for search,
cited Evidence chat, and model-assisted analysis such as OCR and transcription.
Findings remain linked to their sources. Managed hosting is planned.
