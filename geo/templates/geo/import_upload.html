Supported CSV format

Use the export with Asset, Serial Number, Address, Speed, Heading, Trigger, Time Summary, Created Time, and Received Time columns. Each CSV can describe up to {{ max_csv_groups }} distinct Asset and Serial Number pairs. Split exports with more source assets into separate files.

Addresses are retained as reported. Coordinates remain unknown. Serial numbers identify source assets or devices and are not treated as VINs. Time Summary is preserved without assuming a duration or interval.

miTrail lets each user choose their timezone in profile preferences. Match that setting when importing; miTrail does not document one timezone for every account.
