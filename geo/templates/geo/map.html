Evidence files, titles, notes, and source details stay in Pandect. Address lookups send only the address to the configured local geocoder. The background tile service receives only tile requests.

Circles show recorded coordinates; squares show address estimates; diamond markers labelled P show person POIs. POIs are investigator annotations, not retained evidence.
